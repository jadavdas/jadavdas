## Hi there 👋

Robotics & Control Systems Engineer with several years of end-to-end engineering experience, bridging the gap from electromagnetic actuator design to intelligent, full-stack robotic system deployment. I specialize in Model-Based Design (MBD) for both hardware and software-uniting deterministic control theory with modern Machine Learning, Reinforcement Learning (RL), and Physical AI to architect high-reliability cyber-physical systems from Creo/SolidWorks CAD design & FEA, MATLAB/Simulink modeling & simulation and C/C++ code generation & deployment through PyBullet/Simscape multi-physics simulation, hardware-in-the-loop (HIL) testing, and production release.

#### Core Technical Expertise:
- **Physical AI, Reinforcement Learning & Simulation:** Bridging classical dynamics with data-driven intelligence; developing high-fidelity simulation environments in PyBullet to train, validate, and transfer RL control policies (Sim-to-Real) for complex manipulation and locomotion tasks.
- **Model-Based Design & System Architecture:** End-to-end mechanical and mechatronic hardware design in SolidWorks paired with dynamic system modeling, simulation, and code generation in MATLAB/Simulink for seamless hardware-software integration.
- **Actuator & Motor Control:** Ground-up Brushless DC (BLDC) motor design, electromagnetic modeling, servo control, and low-level current, velocity, and position loop tuning.
- **Robotics & Control Theory:** Multi-DoF manipulator kinematics and dynamics, system identification, and the design of linear, nonlinear, and adaptive control algorithms that provide stability guarantees alongside learned policies.
- **Verification, Validation & Quality:** Deterministic HIL testbench architecture, automated software smoke testing, static code analysis, and rigorous V&V protocols to ensure safety-critical performance across both classical and AI-driven stacks.

Whether building custom actuation hardware from scratch or deploying hybrid Model-Based Design and Physical AI architectures on complex manipulators, I thrive on solving multidisciplinary engineering challenges that turn theoretical concepts into robust, real-world physical systems including teleoperated master-slave surgical platform, automated guided vehicles for factory automation, industrial robot for pick-and-place operation of soft and rigid bodies, and humanoid robotics.
Open to connecting with fellow robotics leaders, Physical AI researchers, and organizations building next-generation autonomous platforms.
