## Hi there 👋

I am a Robotics and Control Systems Engineer with a PhD and 20 years of hands-on experience in mechatronics, motor design, and precision motor control. My expertise spans from manipulator design in CAD to develop manipulator forward and inverse kinematics, manipulator dynamics, linear and nonlinear control, adaptive and hybrid control system architecture for precise motion control of the end-effector. Throughout my career spanning both industry and academia, I have built complex hardware systems—including teleoperated master-slave manipulation platforms and custom motor control architectures. Today, I am channeling that deep hardware and control theory foundation into Physical AI, focusing on reinforcement learning, simulation, and control for various robotics application include surgical robots, mobile robots, industrial robot and humanoid robotics. I am passionate about solving hard, purely technical engineering problems that bridge classical mechanics with modern machine learning.
